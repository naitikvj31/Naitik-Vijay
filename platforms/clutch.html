Clutch in detail

What Clutch actually is

A review platform for B2B service providers: agencies, development shops, consultancies. It is not a software review site, which is the usual confusion with G2 and Capterra.

What makes it different is the verification. A Clutch analyst interviews your client by phone or video, or the client completes a verified online review. Either way a real person confirms the project happened. That is why buyers weight it so heavily and why reviews cannot simply be bought.

Clutch also runs the Leaders lists, those “Top Reputation Management Companies” pages that rank on Google for exactly the searches you want. Placement on them is driven by recent review volume, rating, and how complete the profile is.

How the programme runs

Profile first. Services breakdown, project sizes, industries, locations, portfolio entries. Clutch filters buyers by those fields, so an incomplete profile is invisible before it is unconvincing.

Then reviews, from clients who will genuinely take the interview. We identify who, prepare them on what Clutch asks, and schedule it. A client who turns up unprepared gives a thin review, which is worse than none.

Then the lists. Review recency matters more than total count for Leaders placement, so the programme is paced to keep the profile current rather than front-loaded and then left.
