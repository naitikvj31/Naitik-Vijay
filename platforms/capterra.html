Capterra in detail

What Capterra actually is

Capterra is owned by Gartner Digital Markets, along with GetApp and Software Advice. The three sites share one review pool, which means a single approved review can appear in three places at once. That is the whole reason Capterra is such good value.

Capterra listing pages rank unusually well on Google for commercial searches like best project management software. Those are the searches people make right before they choose, and the page is not yours.

What we do on Capterra

  • Full profile buildEvery field completed, categories chosen for where your buyers actually search.
  • Verified reviewer pipelineSourced from your real customers and taken through Capterra's verification.
  • Network syndicationReviews pushed across Capterra, GetApp and Software Advice rather than sitting on one listing.
  • Category positioningIn the lists that rank, not just the ones that sound right.
  • Review responsesOn all three sites, so no profile looks abandoned.
  • Monthly reportingCount, rating and listing position across the network.

How the programme runs

Profile first. Categories, pricing, feature flags, screenshots and the description. Capterra's own filters only surface you if those fields are filled in correctly, so an incomplete profile is invisible before it is unconvincing.

Then verified reviewers from your customer list, at a steady pace, with the incentive declared exactly as Gartner Digital Markets requires.

Then we check syndication. Reviews do not always propagate across all three sites on their own, and chasing that is a real part of the job.
