Product Hunt in detail

What Product Hunt actually is

A daily board where products get posted, upvoted and discussed over a 24 hour window. The leaderboard resets every day, so timing matters enormously.

The part most people miss: after launch day the page keeps existing, keeps getting indexed and keeps ranking for your product name. A good launch buys a spike of traffic and a permanent page one result.

What we do for a launch

  • Launch day planHour by hour, including the first two hours that decide the outcome.
  • Hunter coordinationFinding and briefing someone with reach, where that makes sense for your product.
  • Supporter listReal people lined up in advance, not bought engagement.
  • Assets and copyTagline, gallery, first comment and maker responses prepared before the day.
  • Genuine reviewsFrom people who have actually used the product, after the launch.
  • Post-launch promotionSo the page earns links and keeps ranking for your product name.

How a launch runs

Preparation starts two to three weeks out. Assets, copy, the first comment, the hunter, and a list of people who have genuinely agreed to show up.

On the day, the first two hours decide everything. We plan them hour by hour, because early momentum is what the ranking rewards.

Afterwards we keep the page alive with genuine reviews and comments from people who actually use the product, which is what makes it hold its position in search.
