Paid Reviews & Review Management in detail

The line we will not cross

Every major platform allows incentivised reviews under conditions. The reviewer has to be a genuine, verifiable customer. The incentive has to be declared where the platform asks. Nobody may write the review or ask for a rating.

That is the only model we run. We turn down work that asks for invented reviews, and we will turn down yours. Not out of principle alone: a delisting is a far worse reputation event than the one you came to us with, and it is permanent.

If somebody has quoted you a very low price per review, that is what they are selling you.

What you get

  • G2, Capterra and GartnerThe three that decide B2B software deals, each run to its own rules.
  • Trustpilot and TrustRadiusConsumer trust and detailed B2B evaluations.
  • App Store and Google PlayRating recovery and prompt timing that asks happy users, not annoyed ones.
  • Real customers onlySourced from your list, verified by the platform, never scripted.
  • Replies on every reviewIncluding the bad ones, which is where most of the trust is won.
  • Compliance check firstBefore a single request goes out, so nothing gets pulled down later.

How we work

First we claim and complete your profile, then we check your incentive plan against each platform's current policy. This is the step everyone skips and it is the step that decides whether the programme survives.

Then we run it at a planned pace. Forty reviews landing in one week looks exactly like what it is. Spread properly, the same forty look like a healthy product.

We reply to every incoming review, good and bad. An absent vendor reads worse than a criticised one.
