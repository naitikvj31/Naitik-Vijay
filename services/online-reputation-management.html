Online Reputation Management in detail

What ORM really is

Online reputation management is not deleting bad reviews. Almost nothing on the internet can simply be deleted, and anyone who promises that is selling you something they cannot do.

What it actually is: deciding which ten results should sit on page one for your name, then building, buying and earning those results until they hold. Reviews, press articles, your own pages, paid ads and your social profiles are the raw material. We assemble them.

What you get

  • A full page one mapEvery result for your brand name, scored and categorised, including the ones you cannot see from your own country.
  • Suppression workNew and existing pages built up and promoted until the results you do not want slip off the first page.
  • Review repairResponse on every negative review that is still live, plus a programme that brings in genuine new ones.
  • Press and owned pagesArticles and pages strong enough to hold a top-five spot and keep it.
  • Daily monitoringAlerts on your brand name across search, review sites and social, so nothing surprises you.
  • Clear monthly reportingReview movement and search movement reported separately. No blended averages that hide the slow half.

How we work

Week zero is the consultation, and it is free. We pull every result on page one, score your reviews on each platform against your three closest competitors, and tell you straight what can move and what cannot. If it is a fit we write a scope of work, and nothing is invoiced until you have signed it. You keep the plan either way.

From there we build. Review programmes go live, ads go up on your branded terms, and the first press placements get commissioned. Reviews usually move within four to eight weeks. Search results take three to six months because they depend on new pages earning authority.

Then we defend. Alerts on every surface, a reply on every new review, and a fresh audit every quarter so the next problem gets caught on page three instead of page one.
