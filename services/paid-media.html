Paid Media in detail

Why paid comes first in a reputation problem

Everything else in reputation work takes months. Ads take hours. When a bad result is sitting at position three on your own brand name, the fastest thing you can do is own positions zero and one with paid until the organic work catches up.

It also works the other way. Your competitors' brand names are usually cheap to bid on, and the people searching them are already in buying mode.

What you get

  • Brand defence campaignsYou own position one on your own name, so nobody else can rent it.
  • Competitor conquestAds on the brands you lose deals to, pointed at an honest comparison page.
  • Google, LinkedIn, MetaOne plan across all of them instead of three vendors bidding against each other.
  • Programmatic and retargetingStay in front of the people who looked and left.
  • Landing pages that convertBuilt and tested by us, not handed back to your team as a to-do.
  • Weekly numbersSpend, enquiries, cost per enquiry. In plain language, every week.

How we work

We start with your branded terms, because that is the cheapest traffic you will ever buy and the most expensive to lose. Then comparison and competitor campaigns, then broader intent.

Every campaign points at a landing page we are willing to be judged on. If the page is weak we will say so and fix it, because no amount of bidding rescues a page that does not convert.

Reporting is enquiries and cost per enquiry. Impressions and reach go in the appendix where they belong.
