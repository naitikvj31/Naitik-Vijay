Reputation Consultancy in detail

When consultancy beats a retainer

A retainer makes sense when the work is continuous: review programmes running every month, ads that need managing, press that needs commissioning.

It does not make sense when you have one defined problem and people who can do the work. Paying a monthly fee for something your own team could execute in six weeks is a bad trade and we will say so.

We would rather do a good piece of consultancy and have you come back than sell you twelve months you did not need.

What you get

  • Full reputation auditEvery page one result, every review profile, scored and prioritised.
  • Competitor benchmarkHow your three closest competitors look to the same buyer, and where the gaps are.
  • Crisis playbookWho says what, on which channel, within which hour. Written before you need it.
  • Compliance reviewYour current review and incentive practices checked against each platform's rules.
  • Prioritised action listIn order, with effort and expected impact against each item.
  • A working sessionWe walk your team through all of it live and answer whatever comes up.

How we work

Two to three weeks, fixed scope, fixed price. We audit, we benchmark, we write it up, then we walk your team through it on a call and answer everything.

You own the documents. If you want us to execute afterwards, that is a separate conversation and there is no obligation.
