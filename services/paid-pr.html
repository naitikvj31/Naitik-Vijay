Paid PR & Publications in detail

Pitching versus placing

Traditional PR pitches journalists and hopes. Sometimes it lands, often it does not, and you pay either way.

Paid PR is different. The placement is agreed before you pay for it, so you know the outlet, the format and the date. The article still has to be worth reading, and we write it that way, but the uncertainty is gone.

Both have a place. If your story is genuinely newsworthy we will tell you to pitch it. If you need a result on page one by a certain date, paid placement is the honest answer.

What you get

  • Tier one placementsThe names people recognise, when the story and the budget justify them.
  • Industry and trade pressOften more valuable than the big names, because your actual buyers read them.
  • Founder features and interviewsA face and a story behind the company, which is what investors and senior hires search for.
  • Written for youWe draft it, you approve it. No homework handed back to your marketing team.
  • Promoted after publishingSo the page earns the authority it needs to stay on page one.
  • Guaranteed to runOutlet, format and date agreed before you pay.

How we work

We pick outlets by two things: whether your buyers read them, and whether the page will rank. A huge name that publishes behind a no-index tag does nothing for your search results.

We write the piece or work with your draft. You approve before anything goes live.

Once it publishes we promote it, because a great article nobody links to will not hold a page one spot by itself.
