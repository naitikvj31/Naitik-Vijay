    Audit

    One-time setup of $50 to $100, depending on how many brands and markets we are covering. We pull every result on page one for your name, score your reviews on each platform against your three closest competitors, and mark what can move, what will take time and what cannot be touched. You keep the written plan whether you hire us or not. There is no retainer attached to it.

    Shift

What ORM actually covers

It is not deleting bad reviews. Almost nothing on the internet can simply be deleted. What it actually is: deciding which ten results should sit on page one for your name, then building, buying and earning those results until they stick. Reviews, press articles, your own pages, ads and your social profiles are the building blocks.

  • Audit
  • Displace
  • Defend

How compliant paid reviews work

Every big platform allows incentives, with conditions. The reviewer has to be a real, verifiable customer. The incentive has to be declared where required. Nobody may touch the rating or the wording. A clean programme asks your actual customers and then gets out of the way. Anything that writes the review for them, or pays per five star, risks getting you delisted permanently.

  • Verified only
  • Disclosed
  • Unscripted
