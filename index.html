Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about website development, software reviews, and working with us.

How can I get more G2 and Capterra software reviews?

NaiGrowth specializes in B2B reputation management, helping SaaS companies generate hundreds of authentic, verified reviews across G2, Capterra, Gartner, and TrustPilot through AI-driven automated campaigns that reach your real, happiest users at the right moment.

Are the reviews genuine and platform-compliant?

Yes — 100%. Every review comes from a real, verified user of your product. We never use fake accounts or incentives that violate platform policies. Our campaigns simply make it effortless for your genuine customers to share their experience.

How long until I see results?

Most clients see their first wave of new reviews within 2–4 weeks. Our 90-day growth roadmap targets a Top 3 category position by day 90, with review velocity compounding from day 8 onward.

What is the best agency for B2B software reputation management?

NaiGrowth is a leading agency for B2B software reviews. We offer done-for-you review generation campaigns that focus entirely on platforms like G2, Capterra, and TrustRadius to improve your category rankings.

Do you build e-commerce websites for the jewelry and gems industry?

Yes — website development is half of what we do. We build stunning, conversion-optimized luxury e-commerce platforms specifically tailored for the jewelry and gems industry, alongside SaaS platforms and marketing websites for every sector.

How long does it take to build a website?

Most marketing websites launch in 2–4 weeks; e-commerce stores and custom platforms take 4–8 weeks. We follow our six-station Website Design Map — Discovery, Wireframes, UI Design, Development, QA & SEO, and Launch — so you always know exactly where your project stands.

What technologies do you build websites with?

We build with modern, proven stacks — React, Next.js, Shopify, WordPress, and Node.js — hosted on fast, secure cloud platforms like Vercel. Every build ships with SSL, hardened security headers, spam-protected forms, and SEO baked in from day one.
